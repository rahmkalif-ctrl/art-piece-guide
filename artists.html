Meet the Masters

Discover the legendary artists who transformed visual history through innovation and emotional vision.

Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519)

Movement: High Renaissance | Origin: Italy

A quintessential Renaissance polymath, Da Vinci combined anatomical science, engineering, and art. His mastery of light, subtle transitions, and perspective redefined European painting.

Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890)

Movement: Post-Impressionism | Origin: Netherlands

Creating over 2,100 artworks in just one decade, Van Gogh used bold color and rhythmic brushwork to convey deep emotional states, laying the ground for modern expressionism.

Katsushika Hokusai (1760–1849)

Movement: Ukiyo-e | Origin: Japan

Hokusai was a master woodblock printmaker whose dynamic compositions and dramatic natural scenes deeply influenced Impressionist and Post-Impressionist painters across Europe.
